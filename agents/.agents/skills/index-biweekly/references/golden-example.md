:update: :index: *PROJECT UPDATE*

_TL;DR: Index now captures more customer calls automatically, makes the Gong archive easier to use, connects calls to the right accounts more reliably, and turns important call moments into actionable alerts._

*What shipped in the last two weeks*
* Calls with external participants now record and transcribe automatically, reducing the chance that important customer conversations go uncaptured
* Index is becoming the home for historical calls as Gong sunsets — pasted Gong links now open directly in Index, and the full archive remains searchable
* Calls are more reliably connected to the right account: participants can request association through Slack, and automatic re-association is now live after validating at ~95% precision
* Keyword listeners now turn important moments in Zoom calls into actionable Slack alerts, with the first use cases live
* Index Eve is now available from Athena for call prep and follow-up, while clearer sharing controls, stronger monitoring, and playback recovery make the core experience more dependable

*What's next*
* Turn the call-content encryption and restricted-call privacy scope into an implementation plan
* Expand listeners to more high-value call signals and delivery workflows
* Continue the Gong sunset by making native Zoom capture and the Index archive the complete replacement path
